# Finance automation — Executive Pack

## Outcome and failure analysis
Finance staff should capture once and review exceptions. Current manual recording, independent verification, allocations, expenditure/reconciliation review and audit are working foundations. Re-keying statements, ambiguous references, repeated uploads, inbox noise, hidden sync failure, unsafe bulk posting and unclear revisions are the next friction points. Imported evidence must never silently become bank settlement or approved money. School billing remains separate from SpaceCase subscription billing.

## Product and commercial scope
Offer a useful statement-import workflow without requiring a provider account. Add optional provider, dedicated inbox and accounting connectors by supported capability. Do not promise universal bank connectivity or one-click onboarding where merchant authorization, vendor access, mapping, fees or verification are still needed. Third-party account costs and contracted service levels require a separately chosen provider; no paid service is provisioned by this plan.

Standard experience: role-specific daily brief, saved mappings, explicit source/last-sync status, duplicate and unmatched queues, reasoned suggestions, preview before bulk actions, descriptive exports, mobile forms and recoverable errors. Measure actual imported/processed/exception counts and elapsed work; do not fabricate hours saved or matching accuracy.

## Authority and privacy
School-scoped PEOS permissions remain authoritative on every read/action. Officers prepare, eligible independent staff verify, principals retain policy/expenditure/reconciliation authority. Record bank/provider transaction, reporting, verification, allocation and settlement as distinct facts. No payment initiation/refunds/banking-detail changes through intake. Do not connect general personal inboxes or send imported records to AI. Collect minimum normalized transaction fields; retain provenance without storing whole statement files in the first slice. Retention/evidence download policy is a pre-live gate.

## Delivery gates
1. CSV intake and exception queue; 2. matching/review refinements; 3. one selected payment provider; 4. statements/reminders; 5. expense intake and approval packs; 6. accounting sync. Email and bank-feed adapters follow the connector contract when a pilot source is selected. CSV is first; XLSX and debit/credit-column formats are explicit later capabilities, not implied by CSV support.

Human Preview evidence already obtained: zero-record approval, rejection/revision/resubmission, separate accounts, audit retention/CSV, topic filenames, date auto-advance and physical-phone usability. Sign-out/back navigation remains pending; nonzero imported-money workflow, connector onboarding, principal recovery, retention and production activation remain separate gates.
