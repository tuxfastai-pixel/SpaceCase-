# Parent finance access: first account view

Current slice: ACT-PAY-03-AUTH and ACT-PAY-03-READ. Read-only billing comes before accepted activity plans, private evidence uploads or statements. Preserve the earlier Finance queue in FINANCE_CONTINUITY.md.

PEOS owns a new, separate family-billing-v1 permission. Confirmation of a school contact is deliberately not financial authority. A Principal explicitly checks billing authority for a current confirmed adult/learner/school relationship, chooses expiry and grants read-only access. Self-grants are prohibited. The school relationship, people, active school/enrolment, unexpired grant and current issuer Principal role are checked on every authorization. No PARENT/staff role or unrelated learner permission is assigned. Parent withdrawal and school revocation both stop future access; original records/audits remain. Future plan/proof actions are distinct, not activated by read permission.

SpaceCase consumes strict versioned decisions, current grant/action and short expiry. Client can submit only grant ID for an account read, never authoritative school/learner IDs. The server derives school/learner/person scope from PEOS, reads only that learner's invoices, and rechecks the same current authority before returning data. Issued/allocated totals cover that learner only. No other-family invoice, banking evidence, staff IDs or school-wide totals are projected. Responses are private/no-store; no browser financial persistence. No grant yields an explanation of how to ask the school for permission, not access to a roster.

Principal UI: Review & reconcile → Family billing access. List already-confirmed school contacts, explicit expiry/checked acknowledgement, read-only grant/revoke actions, persistent success notice and saved-contact highlight. No free-form adult or learner identifiers and no automatic grants. Parent UI: Parent workspace → Your school account; choose an authorised learner, view issued fees, verified allocations and outstanding balance. No payment collection, plan commitment, proof upload or statement export is claimed.

Flags default off: PEOS_FAMILY_BILLING_ENABLED (companion), SPACECASE_FAMILY_FINANCE_ENABLED plus the existing Finance operational flag (SpaceCase).
