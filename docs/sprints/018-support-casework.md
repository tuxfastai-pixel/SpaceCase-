# Sprint 018: owned support work and founder escalation
Goal: turn the monitoring desk into a usable support workflow with explicit responsibility.
Scope: distinct canonical SUPPORT_AGENT role; founder grants/revocation; agent claim/release, assigned ticket detail and in-app replies; internal escalations with founder acknowledgement/reassignment; response/request idempotency; queue ownership signals and founder escalation inbox.
Out of scope: external messages, AI investigations, learner database access, billing changes, automatic closure, production repairs and real staff provisioning.
Failure analysis: read-only observers must never inherit response access; agent school scope alone must not reveal arbitrary narratives; concurrent claims must have one winner; retrying a response must not duplicate it; expired/revoked agents must fail on subsequent calls; internal escalation notes must not leak to reporters or observers; assignments must use fresh eligible canonical agent authority.
Ownership is product data in Neon. Identity and school-scoped staff authority remain PEOS-owned. Agent claims/replies/releases/escalations lock the ticket and advance the existing revision; reporter/founder mutations retain their revision conflict protection. Final resolution, proposal approval and execution remain founder-only.
Acceptance: fresh scoped role plus ownership for narrative reads/replies; founder can assign eligible agents and acknowledge escalations; bounded append-only internal history; public replies appear in the reporter's existing timeline; no learner/billing authority added.
Validation: role/input tests, school isolation, owner-only narrative access, competing claims, idempotent writes, internal-note privacy and full CI builds. Migration must pass isolated Neon/runtime checks before production confirmation. No signed-in browser claim without an authenticated session.
