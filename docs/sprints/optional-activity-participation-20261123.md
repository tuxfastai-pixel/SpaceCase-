# Optional activity participation — 23 November 2026

Goal: remove the circular requirement for a pre-existing invoice before a family can choose an optional activity. Finance explicitly offers one independently approved/published optional activity to a checked current school learner. An authorised billing contact then reviews the fixed amount/date and accepts or declines. Acceptance atomically creates one invoice, line, due-date instalment, activity-charge link and immutable participation audit. Decline creates no invoice. Existing invoice-linking remains available for already-agreed charges.

PEOS remains canonical for relationships and purpose-specific permission. New `family.finance.activity.accept` is separate from read/plan/proof authority and is never inferred from an old grant. Principal explicitly checks the permission; existing grants must be revoked/replaced through the existing reviewed grant flow. No automatic grants. Parent HTTP cannot supply another learner/school or a price. Current scope is rechecked before commit, school account is locked, and unique learner/activity offers + unique offer decisions prevent duplicate charges across retries/two family contacts. Changed/cancelled/unpublished/past-deadline activities cannot be accepted. Prior outcomes remain readable after cancellation while current billing access remains required. No receipts, allocations, payment verification, messages or attendance claims are created.

Scope: optional activities only; explicit individual eligibility, never audience-text inference or blanket class charges. Saved decline/accept choices require school assistance to change; no automatic refunds/voids. New feature gate `SPACECASE_OPTIONAL_ACTIVITY_PARTICIPATION_ENABLED` defaults off and requires the existing family-plan/register/finance gates. New additive product migration and companion action-set migration must precede isolated activation. Notifications and automatic statement policies remain separate unfinished work. Private proof/worker collection remains off.
