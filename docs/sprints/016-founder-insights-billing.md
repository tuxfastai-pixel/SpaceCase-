# Sprint 016: founder measurements and billing records
Goal: give the founder measured product/support signals and a safe school billing ledger.
Scope: founder snapshot and thresholded pattern groups; founder invoice/verified receipt entry, disputes, reversals and voiding; school-admin read-only billing; published billing preparation explanation; operations staffing and staged scale design.
Out of scope: payment collection, tax invoice issuance, penalties, suspension, legally finalized contracts, hired observer accounts, AI investigations, automated experiments and external introductions.
Acceptance: metrics describe actual sources and unknowns; school admins see only their school ledger; teachers cannot access billing; receipts never exceed outstanding invoice amount; duplicates don't double-charge; no service access changes.
Privacy: no learner-level analytics, no ticket narratives in patterns, no bank/card details.
Required checks: CI typecheck/lint/tests/build, Neon branch migration/runtime permission probe, production confirmation before dependent release. No claim of 10k–15k-school capacity without load evidence.
