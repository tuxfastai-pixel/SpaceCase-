The activity register's audience text cannot establish learner eligibility. Finance must explicitly check eligibility and link a published activity version to an already-issued invoice for the same school/account and learner, exact amount and funding deadline. One invoice cannot be linked twice; the same learner/activity cannot acquire a second linked invoice through a retry. This service creates no invoice or event attendance consent. Optional activity enrolment/charge issuance and broader staff permissions remain separate work.

An accepted plan stores an append-only school-year revision: actor/grant, school-account/learner scope, reviewed invoice balances, published activity version, school calendar/policy, exact instalments or Flexi acknowledgement, and acceptance time. A newer school calendar or cancellation does not rewrite an older agreement. New acceptance uses the policy explicitly attached to the still-published activity; a later calendar alone does not silently withdraw that offer. Cancellation prevents new acceptance while preserving prior agreements. Revisions share one learner/account/academic-year sequence even when the calendar version changes. Existing invoice debts remain independent of plan selection. A plan is a contribution intention, not a receipt, verified payment, new debt or bank settlement.

The service requires current family.finance.plan.accept authority, independently of read access, and rechecks the same grant/person/school/learner/action before committing, including retries. The server loads invoice links and balances under the existing account lock; a browser can select a charge ID and its reviewed expected balance but cannot supply an authoritative learner, school, price or allocation. A changed balance requires a fresh review. Request IDs bind the exact validated request, actor and grant; identical retry returns the original record after current authorization, while conflicting/stale submissions fail. Two concurrent revisions cannot both win. Runtime tables allow SELECT/INSERT only; history cannot be updated or deleted through that role. No access grant is seeded.

Fixed schedules must sum exactly to the selected outstanding amount, meet the school minimum and cover every activity funding deadline. Terms and half-years use the saved school ranges. Monthly/every-two-month dates retain the selected day, clamped in short months; custom amounts/dates require the school's CUSTOM option. Past dates use the server's Africa/Johannesburg school day. Flexi requires an explicit deadline acknowledgement and carries visible shortfalls; it does not fabricate scheduled payments. Early/top-up contributions and verified receipt allocation remain separate workflows and never silently rewrite the accepted plan.
